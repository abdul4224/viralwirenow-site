About ViralWireNow

ViralWireNow is an online news publication covering world news, artificial intelligence, technology, business and science. Our goal is simple: explain what happened, why it matters and what to watch next, in plain language and without hype.

How our articles are made

We use an AI-assisted workflow with built-in editorial safeguards. It works in five steps:

  1. Discovery. We monitor public news feeds and search trends to find stories people are following.
  2. Research. A story is only written when at least two different publishers have reported real facts about it. If the source material is thin, the story is skipped instead of being padded.
  3. Writing. AI drafts the article strictly from those reported facts, attributes claims to the outlets that made them, and is instructed never to invent names, numbers, dates or quotes.
  4. Automated checks. Every draft passes safety, originality, sourcing and quality gates before it can be published. Drafts that fail are held back.
  5. Publication and corrections. Each article lists its sources. If a reader finds a mistake we fix it publicly - see our Corrections Policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not copy other publishers' articles. We summarise reported facts in our own words and link to the original reporting.
  • We do not present stock photos as photos of the event. Images are labelled as illustrative.
  • We do not publish anonymous rumours as fact, or accept payment for coverage.

Who is behind ViralWireNow

ViralWireNow is an independent project. The editorial process, source rules and safeguards described here are set and maintained by the site team, who also handle reader feedback and corrections. Read the full standards in our Editorial Policy.

Funding

The site is supported by advertising. Ads are clearly separated from editorial content and never influence what we cover. See the Disclaimer for details.
