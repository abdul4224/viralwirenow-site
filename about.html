About

ViralWireNow covers technology, business, science, and world news, combining automated trend discovery with AI-assisted writing and human-designed editorial safeguards.

Every article starts from multiple public news sources. Our system extracts the underlying facts, cross-checks them for consistency, and only proceeds to writing when there's enough independently reported information to work from. If a topic doesn't have enough verifiable source material, it's held back rather than published with guesswork filled in.

Before anything goes live, each article passes through an originality check (to catch anything too close to its sources), a content-safety check (to catch unsupported claims or unsafe topics), and an advertising-policy check (to keep prohibited categories off pages that carry ads). Anything that fails is held for review rather than published automatically.

Video content on our associated YouTube channel is produced with AI-assisted narration and editing, and that involvement is disclosed in each video's description.
