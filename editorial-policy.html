Editorial Policy

This policy explains the standards every ViralWireNow article is held to. We publish it so readers can judge our work by our own rules.

1. Accuracy and sourcing

  • An article is only produced when at least two independent publishers have reported the underlying facts.
  • Specific claims are attributed to the outlet that reported them (for example, "according to BBC News"). Each article ends with a list of its sources.
  • We never invent quotes, statistics, dates, names or events. Where reports differ or a detail is unconfirmed, we say so.
  • Breaking stories can change quickly. We describe what is known at the time of writing and update articles when reliable new information appears.

2. Use of artificial intelligence

We openly use AI to help draft articles from the reported facts collected in the research step. AI is a writing tool inside a controlled process, not a source of facts. Automated checks review each draft for unsupported claims, sensitive content, copied text and thin content, and drafts that fail are not published.

3. Originality and copyright

We write in our own words. We do not reproduce other publishers' articles, and we keep any quotation short and attributed. Original reporters and outlets are credited and linked in the Sources section.

4. Images and video

Where we do not have a photograph of the actual event, we use licensed stock imagery and label it as illustrative. Our short video summaries are produced from the same article text and are labelled as summaries.

5. Sensitive topics

We take extra care with stories involving deaths, crime, health, elections and conflict: we avoid speculation, do not name unverified suspects and do not publish medical or financial advice. Health articles are informational only.

6. Independence and advertising

Advertising is placed in clearly separated ad slots. Advertisers have no influence over which stories we cover or how we cover them. We do not sell coverage.

7. Corrections

When we get something wrong we correct it promptly and transparently. See the Corrections Policy.

8. Feedback

We welcome feedback on accuracy, fairness and tone via the Contact page.
