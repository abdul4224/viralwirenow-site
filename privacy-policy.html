Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information ViralWireNow ("we", "us") collects when you visit https://viralwirenow.com, how it is used, and your choices.

Information we collect

We do not require accounts and do not ask you for personal information to read the site. Like almost every website, the servers that deliver our pages automatically receive technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested and the time of the request.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our own pages do not set tracking cookies. Third-party services we use (advertising and embedded video, described below) may set cookies or use similar technologies to deliver and measure content. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; parts of third-party content may then not work.

Advertising

We display advertisements provided by Adsterra, a third-party advertising network. Advertising partners may collect information such as your IP address, browser and device information and pages viewed, and may use cookies to show ads and measure their performance. Their handling of data is described in their own privacy policy. We do not share personal information such as your name or email address with advertisers.

Embedded content and share links

Some articles embed videos from YouTube. Video is loaded only when you choose to play it. When you play it, YouTube may collect data under Google's privacy policy. Our "share" buttons are ordinary links: no data is sent to a social network unless you click one.

Search Console and analytics

We use Google Search Console, which reports how our pages appear in Google Search. It does not identify individual visitors. If we add an analytics service we will update this policy first.

Email you send us

If you contact us, we use your email address and message only to reply and to handle your request, and we keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR or California under the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, to object to certain processing, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Because we hold almost no personal data, most requests concern our advertising partners; you can contact us and we will help you reach the right party. You can also use your browser's privacy settings or industry opt-out tools to limit ad personalisation.

Children

ViralWireNow is a general news site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to other sites

Our articles link to outside publishers. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use our Contact page.
