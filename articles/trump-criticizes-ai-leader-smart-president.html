In a recent critique, US President Donald Trump has lambasted an AI company leader for advocating a slowdown in artificial intelligence (AI) development. Trump's Criticism of AI Slowdown

The debate over AI regulation has been heating up, with concerns about the rapid advancement of AI and its potential risks. A leader from an AI firm suggested that the development of AI should be slowed down to mitigate these risks. However, President Trump has strongly opposed this notion, arguing that the solution lies not in slowing down the technology, but in having a 'smart president' who can make informed decisions and guide the development and use of AI.
