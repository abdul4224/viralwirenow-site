Kennedy Center Board Votes to Close Venue for Trump-Pushed Renovations

2026-09-15 · By ViralWireNow Editorial

In a move that has sparked debate and controversy, the board of the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts, which was appointed by former President Donald Trump, has voted to immediately close the venue for extensive renovations. This decision comes after a chaotic call with Trump, as reported by CNN, and follows a court filing that suggested the meeting would focus exclusively on closure.

Decision to Close

The board's decision to close the Kennedy Center was made at a meeting that reportedly turned hostile and conflicted, as highlighted in a The Hill report. The move aims to address long-standing issues with the venue's facilities, but it has raised questions about the timing and the board's motivations.
