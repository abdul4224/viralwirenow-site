Key takeaways

  • Goodman Group has scrapped plans for a $1.2bn datacentre in Sydney’s Lane Cove due to emerging regulations from state and federal governments.
  • The developer cites significant changes in the policy and regulatory environment for large-scale datacentres.
  • The decision comes after the plans were withdrawn from the NSW planning portal.

Developer Goodman Group has scrapped plans for a controversial $1.2bn datacentre in Lane Cove, Sydney, citing 'emerging' regulations from state and federal governments. The plans, known as 'Project Mars', were withdrawn from the NSW planning portal on Monday, with Goodman Group posting on its website that the policy and regulatory environment for large-scale datacentres had 'evolved significantly'.

What Happened

Goodman Group, a major property developer, announced the cancellation of the $1.2bn datacentre project, known as 'Project Mars', in Lane Cove. The decision was made after the company identified significant changes in the regulatory landscape, which it believes have made the project less viable. The plans were withdrawn from the New South Wales (NSW) planning portal, marking the end of the project. This move comes as a surprise to many in the tech and real estate industries, as the project was seen as a significant opportunity for both economic growth and technological advancement in the region.

Why It Matters

The cancellation of the datacentre project has significant implications for the tech industry and local economy in Sydney. The project was expected to create thousands of jobs and contribute to the growth of the datacentre sector in the region. By pulling the plug, Goodman Group highlights the growing importance of regulatory compliance in large-scale infrastructure projects.

The decision also underscores the evolving nature of datacentre regulations, which are likely to continue shaping the industry in the coming years. The tech industry in Sydney is already facing challenges, such as high operational costs and competition from other major cities. The cancellation of the Mars project could further impact the sector's ability to attract new investments and talent.

Background

Project Mars was a highly anticipated development in the Sydney tech landscape, aimed at providing state-of-the-art datacentre facilities. The project was expected to be one of the largest in Australia, with a capacity to house over 100,000 servers. The plans faced opposition from environmental groups and local residents, who were concerned about the potential impact on the local community and the environment.

The project's cancellation comes as the government is increasingly focused on regulating the datacentre sector to ensure sustainability and compliance with environmental standards. The cancellation of the Mars project is a clear indication of the government's commitment to addressing these concerns.

Goodman Group has not provided specific details on the nature of the emerging regulations that led to the cancellation. The decision to cancel the project has not affected other Goodman Group developments, and the company remains committed to other infrastructure projects in the region.
