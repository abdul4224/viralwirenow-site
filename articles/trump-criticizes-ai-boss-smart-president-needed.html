Trump Criticizes AI Boss: Only 'Smart President' Needed

2026-09-14 · By ViralWireNow Editorial

In a recent development, President Donald Trump criticized the head of an artificial intelligence company for calling for a slowdown in AI development. Instead, Trump argues that the only necessary guardrail is a 'smart president' capable of overseeing advancements in the field.

Trump's Criticism

In several statements, Trump has criticized the AI industry, particularly the calls for slowing down its development. He argues that a 'smart president' is all that’s needed to regulate AI, as opposed to implementing additional guardrails or regulations.

FAQ

What did Trump criticize about the AI industry?

Trump criticized the calls for slowing down AI development and suggested that only a 'smart president' is necessary to regulate the field.

Conclusion

Trump's stance highlights the ongoing debate over the role of government in regulating artificial intelligence. While some advocate for strict guidelines, others, like Trump, believe that the responsibility lies with competent leadership.
