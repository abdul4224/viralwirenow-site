In a significant victory for voting rights advocates, the Supreme Court has upheld the constitutionality of mail-in voting in a ruling that rejected former President Donald Trump’s attempts to impose restrictions on mail-in ballots. This decision ensures that millions of American citizens can continue to exercise their right to vote by mail without facing new obstacles.

Supreme Court's Ruling

The high court’s decision comes in response to a challenge from the Trump administration that sought to limit the number of mail-in ballots. The court’s ruling supports the existing federal rules that allow for expanded mail voting, particularly in light of the pandemic which necessitated the increase in mail-in ballots.
