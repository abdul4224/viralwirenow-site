Trump’s Failed Attempt to Make Canada the 51st State: Now Pushing for EU Association

2026-09-17 · By ViralWireNow Editorial

Former President Donald Trump's ambitious plan to make Canada the 51st state in the United States has faced numerous setbacks. Instead of realizing this dream, Trump has now turned his attention to the European Union. In a series of threats and public statements, Trump has warned the EU not to pursue associate membership for Canada, threatening heavy tariffs if the plan moves forward.

What Happened

In 2017, Donald Trump expressed interest in making Canada the 51st state of the United States, a move that was seen as a way to bolster the American economy and strengthen the relationship between the two countries. However, this idea faced significant opposition, particularly from the Canadian government and the American public. As a result, the idea was largely abandoned. Fast forward to 2023, Trump has now shifted his focus to the European Union. In a series of public statements, he has warned the EU that if they proceed with associate membership for Canada, the United States will impose 'very heavy tariffs.'

Why It Matters

The implications of Trump's statements are significant. The United States and Canada share a complex and interdependent trade relationship, and any disruption could have far-reaching economic consequences. Additionally, the move to the European Union could impact the balance of power in international trade, potentially leading to trade wars and economic instability.

Official Response

The European Union has shown no signs of backing down. In an article from The Washington Post, it was noted that the EU has a history of misadventures in its pursuit of associating with countries like Canada. The EU has stated that it sees no reason to abandon its plans and has called Trump's threats 'hostile.'

What Happens Next

The situation remains tense, with the European Union showing no signs of backing down. The outcome of this political standoff could have significant implications for international trade and economic relations. The next few weeks and months will likely be crucial in determining whether Canada will pursue its EU association plans, and how the United States will respond.
