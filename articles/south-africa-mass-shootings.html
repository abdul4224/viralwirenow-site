At least 27 people were killed in two mass shootings in South Africa, according to police reports. The incidents occurred in bars or taverns near Johannesburg and Cape Town, as the country grapples with one of the world’s highest murder rates. The South African police service said on Sunday that 17 people were killed on Saturday night when eight suspects armed with AK-47 assault rifles and pistols opened fire at a bar in Wedela township, about 50 miles south-west of central Johannesburg.

At least 15 other people sustained gunshot injuries in that attack. On Saturday night, there were separate mass shootings near South Africa's two largest cities, leaving 10 people dead in a separate incident at a barbecue venue near Cape Town. These latest incidents come as the country struggles to stop high levels of gang-related armed violence, a problem that has plagued the nation for years.

What Happened

On Saturday night, two mass shootings occurred near South Africa's two largest cities. The second incident involved 10 people being killed at a barbecue venue near Cape Town. The shootings are believed to be related to illegal gold mining and business rivalries, according to the South African police service.
